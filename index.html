About

Thoughtful audio work for artists, ensembles, and students

I work at the intersection of music, engineering, and teaching. My background includes recording and mixing, music production, live and ensemble-oriented work, and higher education. I care about strong results, clear communication, and helping good music sound like itself at its best.

Professional and human

Whether you are finishing a release, building a portfolio, or leading a community-based project, my goal is the same: do the work well, make the process clear, and deliver something worth hearing.

Clear process

I value communication, organization, and a workflow people can actually follow. Good engineering should feel intentional, not chaotic.

Musical judgment

I care about musical results, not just technical polish. The point is not to overwork a recording. The point is to help it sound like itself at its best.

Teaching

Educator's perspective

Teaching is an important part of my professional life, and it shapes the way I approach audio work. I care about helping students and developing engineers understand not just what to do, but why it works.

Teaching philosophy

I want students to develop strong ears, strong habits, and strong judgment. Technical knowledge matters, but so does taste, organization, and the ability to explain your decisions clearly.

What matters to me

I care a lot about craft. I like work that is thoughtful, honest, and structurally sound. Whether I am mixing a song, recording an ensemble, or teaching students how to evaluate what they are hearing, I am interested in the same basic question: what makes this actually good, and how do we get there on purpose?
